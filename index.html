About

Born in the backwaters. Building for them.

I grew up in Kainakary — a small village in Alleppey surrounded by canals, paddy fields, and houseboats. The Kerala backwaters were never a tourist destination for me. They were simply home.

That local experience eventually became the foundation for Backwater Holidays , a Kerala houseboat booking platform focused on verified operators, transparent pricing, and more honest travel experiences.

Alongside tourism, I work in full-stack web development, SEO architecture, performance optimisation, and digital product building — especially for travel-focused businesses.

The Startup

Why I built Backwater Holidays

Every tourism season I watched travellers arrive in Alleppey expecting one experience and receiving another. Misleading pricing, recycled photographs, and aggregator-driven bookings created a gap between what Kerala actually offers and what people were being sold online.

I built Backwater Holidays to make Kerala houseboat booking more transparent, local-first, and trustworthy.

The platform connects travellers directly with verified operators across Alleppey and Kerala's backwaters while keeping the experience authentic to the place itself.

Development

Building with intention.

My work focuses on full-stack development, booking systems, semantic SEO, travel technology, performance optimisation, and scalable tourism infrastructure.

Most of what I learned came through building live products, solving real problems, and continuously improving systems based on how actual users behave.
