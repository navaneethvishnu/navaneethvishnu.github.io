About

Born in the backwaters.
Building for them.

I grew up in Kainakary — a small island village in Alleppey district, surrounded entirely by water. The backwaters were not a tourist attraction for me. They were where I swam as a child, where my family fished, where neighbours moved between houses by boat on monsoon mornings.

That upbringing gave me something no travel guide can manufacture: a genuine understanding of how the Kerala backwaters actually work — which operators are honest, which routes are genuinely beautiful, and what a traveller really needs versus what they are often sold.

I taught myself to code as a teenager, drawn equally to both worlds: the water outside and the screen inside. Over time, I realised the two could be unified. The gap between how Kerala's houseboat tourism actually operates and how it is presented online to travellers is enormous. I wanted to close it.

"There is a version of Alleppey that belongs to brochures. And then there is the real one — quieter, more beautiful, harder to find."

— Navaneeth Vishnu

The Project

Backwater
Holidays

A verified booking platform for Kerala houseboats. Built from the ground up to solve a real problem I watched unfold every season.

Kerala Tourism Houseboat Booking Alleppey Travel Tech

Visit the platform: backwaterholidays.in — Kerala's local-first houseboat booking experience.

Every season I watched travellers arrive in Alleppey clutching printouts from booking aggregators, expecting one houseboat and getting another. I watched commissions swallow operators. I watched pricing games play out at the jetty. The Kerala houseboat market, rich and beautiful as it is, had a transparency problem.

In 2022 I started building Backwater Holidays — not as a startup idea pulled from a pitch deck, but as an answer to something I lived around. The goal was straightforward: let travellers book directly with local, verified operators across the Alleppey backwaters, with honest pricing, real photographs, and no intermediary markup eroding the experience at both ends.

The platform handles search, real-time availability, route customisation — overnight cruises through Punnamada Lake, dawn departures from Kumarakom, private charters into the inner canal villages — and supports operators in managing their bookings without being swallowed by platform fees they can't sustain.

Backwater Holidays is built by someone from here, for travellers who want to actually experience Kerala rather than a packaged simulation of it.

Most of what I have learned came not from a classroom but from building something live, watching it break, and fixing it with better understanding each time.

Purpose

Why this
site exists

Personal founder sites should do one thing well: make it clear who you are and why your work matters.

01

Honest information exists

Most travel content about the Kerala backwaters is written at a distance or written to rank. Someone who grew up on these waters needs to make the accurate version available too.

02

Local founders deserve a voice

Technology built for a place by someone from that place is different in quality. It accounts for context that outsiders building from data alone cannot see. I want that perspective to be legible.

03

Travellers deserve direct access

When a traveller books through Backwater Holidays, they are booking directly with a Kerala operator I have personally verified. That relationship should not be buried three aggregator layers deep.

04

Kerala tourism deserves better infrastructure

The backwaters are one of the most extraordinary places in the world. The digital infrastructure that represents them to global travellers should be worthy of that.
