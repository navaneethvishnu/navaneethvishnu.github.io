Origin

A Kerala kid who
learned to code and build.

I grew up in Kainakary — a small island village tucked into the Vembanad lake system in Alleppey. Most people don't know Kainakary by name, but they know the feeling: still water, coconut palms leaning into the sky, wooden boats threading through narrow canals. That was my childhood.

What most visitors to Kerala experience as a romantic escape was, for me, ordinary life. The houseboats drifting past my window weren't novelties — they were neighbors. The backwaters weren't a destination; they were a geography I understood from the inside.

As I got older, I became interested in how the internet worked — not just as a user, but as a builder. I taught myself web development, understood how search engines ranked content, and eventually combined both worlds: the local knowledge I'd grown up with and the technical ability to put it online properly.

This is where Backwater Holidays came from. Not from a business school plan. From a real place, and a real frustration with how badly Kerala's tourism technology was failing both travelers and local operators.

The venture

Why I built
Backwater Holidays.

Kerala's tourism economy is built on houseboats — particularly in the Alleppey district, where the backwater network is one of the most photographed landscapes in Asia. Yet for years, the booking experience was a mess. Travelers relied on middlemen, outdated portals, and misleading listings to find and book a houseboat. Prices were inflated. Quality was unpredictable. Local operators — many of them families who had run boats for generations — lost margin to aggregators who had no connection to the water at all.

I saw this problem firsthand. As someone from Kainakary, I knew the operators. I knew the boats. I knew the difference between a genuine experience on Vembanad lake and a tourist-trap version of the same route. That knowledge felt useful — if I could bring it online in a way that actually worked.

So I built Backwater Holidays — a clean, fast, honest houseboat booking platform designed around what travelers actually need: verified boats, real pricing, and direct access to local operators who care about their guests. No fluff. No fake reviews padded by middlemen.

"The best way to book a Kerala houseboat is directly — with someone who knows the water. That's what Backwater Holidays is built to enable."

Why this site exists

Real information,
from a real place.

There's no shortage of content about Kerala travel. What's missing is content from people who actually live here, who know the operators by name, and who have a stake in travelers having a genuine experience rather than a manufactured one.

  • First-hand perspective Everything I write about the Kerala backwaters comes from lived experience in Kainakary and Alleppey — not research from behind a desk.
  • No affiliate incentives When I recommend something, it's because I think it's genuinely good. My business is Backwater Holidays — I don't need affiliate commissions from unrelated sources.
  • Helping local operators Kerala's houseboat industry is full of excellent, honest operators who are invisible online. Part of what I do is fix that — through technology and through content.
  • Against misinformation There's a lot of misleading content about Kerala travel. Outdated pricing, fake reviews, unreliable guides. I try to be a corrective to that.

The backwaters of Kerala are one of the most extraordinary environments on earth. They deserve to be represented by people who understand them — not by platforms optimizing for clicks.
